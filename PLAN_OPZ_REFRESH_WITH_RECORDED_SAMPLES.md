## Hard constraints

- No OS/firmware or bootloader update as part of this refresh; no NAND-format command (`0x15`). A narrowly scoped engines-only repair is allowed only at the gated step above with explicit approval and evidence of the CRC failure.
- Never host-write `settings/plugs.json` or `settings/slotConfiguration.json`; the official app/device owns those bindings.
- Import sample audio only through the documented content-disk slot importer. Do not rely on updater writes or `samplepacks/user/` as the sample-audio import route.
- Do not delete valid device-generated dedup markers or create duplicate declarations; do not overwrite a currently working pack without a host-side backup and an approved rollback.
- No bulk install before the representative engine and pack pass their gates. Do not call substitutes authentic factory audio or claim “as new” while any acceptance check is unresolved.

## Acceptance criteria

A full result requires all 19 intended assets to have verified roles/formats and working device-side bindings, all 21 official TE engines to be correctly installed through valid mappings, and the user's projects/tracks to boot, sound, and run under PLAY without errors. Registered sample memory must stay below 24 MB with measured headroom. The engine/sample state must persist through content-mode eject and reboot. If `CuckooC_keypawn` cannot be mapped/formatted correctly, an engine cannot be persisted, or PLAY still hangs, report the concrete partial result instead of claiming a complete refresh.
